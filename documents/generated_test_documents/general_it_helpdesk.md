# IT Helpdesk and Technology Support at Nexora Technologies

## Getting Help

The IT Helpdesk is the first point of contact for all technology-related issues. You can reach the IT Helpdesk through:

- **Email**: ithelpdesk@nexora.tech (response within 4 hours during business hours)
- **Slack channel**: #it-support (for non-urgent queries)
- **Phone**: Extension 5555 (for urgent issues during working hours)
- **Walk-in support**: IT desk on the 3rd floor of the Hyderabad office (10 AM to 5 PM)

## Common IT Requests

### Password Reset
- Use the self-service password reset portal at passwords.nexora.tech.
- If locked out, contact IT Helpdesk with your employee ID.
- Passwords must be changed every **90 days** and meet complexity requirements (8+ characters, uppercase, lowercase, number, special character).

### Software Installation
- All software must be requested through the IT portal.
- Approved software is installed within **2 working days**.
- Unauthorized software installation is a violation of the IT security policy.

### VPN Access
- VPN is required for accessing company systems from outside the office network.
- VPN credentials are provided during onboarding.
- If VPN is not working, restart the VPN client and try connecting again before contacting IT.

### Hardware Issues
- Report hardware issues (laptop not working, broken keyboard, display problems) through the IT portal.
- Replacement hardware is provided within **3 working days** for critical issues.
- Employees are responsible for taking care of assigned equipment.

## Information Security Basics

- Never share your login credentials with anyone, including colleagues and managers.
- Lock your laptop (Windows+L) when stepping away from your desk.
- Do not connect personal USB devices to company laptops.
- Report any suspicious emails or phishing attempts to security@nexora.tech.
- Use only company-approved cloud storage (Google Workspace, company SharePoint) for work files.
- Two-factor authentication (2FA) is mandatory for all company systems.

## Company-Provided Equipment

Every employee receives the following during onboarding:
- Laptop (Dell or MacBook based on role)
- Wireless mouse and keyboard
- Laptop bag
- Monitor (for employees working from office regularly)

All equipment must be returned in working condition during the exit process.

# Office Facilities and FAQ — Nexora Technologies

## Office Location

Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. is headquartered at:
- **Address**: Tower B, Nexora Tech Park, HITEC City, Hyderabad, Telangana 500081
- **Branch Office**: Nexora Innovation Hub, Koramangala, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560034

## Office Timings and Access

- Office is accessible from **7:00 AM to 10:00 PM** on weekdays.
- Weekend access requires prior approval from your department head.
- After-hours access requires a security request raised through the Facilities portal.

## Cafeteria

- Open **8:30 AM to 7:30 PM** on weekdays.
- Subsidized breakfast (Rs. 30), lunch (Rs. 50), and snacks (Rs. 20) available.
- Special dietary options (vegetarian, vegan, Jain) are available daily.
- Tea and coffee vending machines are available on every floor, free of charge.

## Parking

- Two-wheeler parking: Basement Level B1 (free).
- Four-wheeler parking: Basement Level B2 (Rs. 500/month, deducted from salary).
- Register your vehicle at the Security desk with your employee ID and vehicle registration number.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How many paid leaves do I get per year?**
A: Every full-time employee receives 24 paid leave days per calendar year.

**Q: How many sick leaves do I get per year?**
A: Every employee receives 12 sick leave days per calendar year.

**Q: Can I carry forward my unused paid leave?**
A: Yes, up to 10 unused paid leave days can be carried forward to the next year.

**Q: Can I work from home?**
A: Eligible employees who have completed probation may request up to 8 WFH days per month.

**Q: What is the notice period?**
A: The standard notice period is 60 calendar days. During probation, it is 30 days.

**Q: How do I raise an IT support request?**
A: Email ithelpdesk@nexora.tech, call extension 5555, or post in the #it-support Slack channel.

**Q: What is the annual bonus range?**
A: The standard bonus range is 5% to 20% of annual base salary for eligible employees.

**Q: How do I claim professional development reimbursement?**
A: Submit a request through the HR portal with course details and manager approval. The annual limit is Rs. 40,000.

# New Employee Onboarding Guide — Nexora Technologies

## Welcome to Nexora Technologies!

Congratulations on joining Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. This guide will help you navigate your first few weeks at the company.

## Day 1 Checklist

Your first day at Nexora includes:

1. **Reporting time**: 10:00 AM at the Hyderabad headquarters, Reception Desk (Ground Floor).
2. **ID card and access badge**: Issued by the Security team after photo verification.
3. **Laptop and equipment**: Collected from the IT desk (3rd Floor) after completing the IT onboarding form.
4. **HR orientation session**: 11:00 AM to 12:30 PM covering company policies, benefits, and compliance.
5. **Team introduction**: Your manager will introduce you to your team and assign a buddy.
6. **Lunch with buddy**: Your assigned buddy will take you for lunch to help you settle in.

## First Week

During your first week, you will:

- Complete mandatory compliance training modules on the Learning Management System (LMS).
- Set up your email, Slack, HR portal, and other company tool accounts.
- Meet with your manager to discuss your 30-60-90 day plan.
- Attend a product overview session to understand Nexora's products and services.
- Complete your bank details and tax declaration forms on the HR portal.

## Probation Period

- All new employees are on a **6-month probation period**.
- During probation, the notice period is **30 days** (instead of the standard 60 days).
- A probation review meeting happens at the end of Month 3 and Month 6.
- Successful completion of probation is confirmed in writing by HR.
- During probation, WFH privileges are limited to **4 days per month**.

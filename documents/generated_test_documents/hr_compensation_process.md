# Compensation Bands and Salary Structure — HR Confidential

**Classification: HR Confidential — Sharing compensation band details with employees outside of their own band is a policy violation.**

## Compensation Philosophy

Nexora Technologies aims to pay competitively at the **60th percentile** of the Hyderabad technology market. Compensation is reviewed annually using data from Mercer, Aon, and Glassdoor benchmarking surveys.

## Salary Bands by Level

The following are the annual CTC (Cost to Company) ranges for each job level at Nexora Technologies:

|-------|-------|----------------------|
| L1 | Associate Engineer / Analyst | Rs. 4,00,000 — Rs. 7,00,000 |

| L4 | Staff Engineer / Manager | Rs. 14,00,000 — Rs. 22,00,000 |
| L5 | Senior Manager / Principal Engineer | Rs. 20,00,000 — Rs. 32,00,000 |

## CTC Breakdown

The standard CTC structure for all levels is:

- **Basic Salary**: 40% of CTC
- **House Rent Allowance (HRA)**: 20% of CTC
- **Special Allowance**: 15% of CTC
- **Performance Bonus (Variable Pay)**: 10-15% of CTC
- **Employer PF Contribution**: 12% of Basic Salary
- **Health Insurance (Employer Contribution)**: As per plan
- **Gratuity**: As per statutory requirements

## Salary Review Cycle

- Annual salary reviews are conducted in **April** each year.
- Salary increments are linked to performance ratings:
  - Rating 5: **15-25%** increment
  - Rating 4: **10-15%** increment
  - Rating 3: **6-10%** increment
  - Rating 2: **0-3%** increment
  - Rating 1: **No increment** (placed on PIP)

## Off-Cycle Adjustments

Off-cycle salary adjustments may be approved in exceptional cases such as:
- Significant change in role scope or responsibility.
- Retention cases where an employee has received a competitive external offer.
- Market corrections when benchmark data shows significant underpayment.

Off-cycle adjustments require approval from the VP of HR and the employee's business unit head.

## Confidentiality

Compensation information is classified as HR Confidential. Employees should not discuss specific salary figures with colleagues. Violations of compensation confidentiality are treated as a code of conduct violation.

# Department Budget Allocations FY 2026-27 — Management Confidential

**Classification: Management Confidential — For people managers and above only. Do not share with individual contributors.**

## Budget Overview

The total approved operating budget for Nexora Technologies for FY 2026-27 (April 2026 — March 2027) is **Rs. 85.4 Crores**. This represents a **12% increase** over FY 2025-26.

## Budget Guidelines for Managers

1. **Recurring Expenses**: Any new recurring expense above **Rs. 20 Lakhs per year** requires VP sign-off.
2. **Capital Expenditure**: Hardware and infrastructure purchases above **Rs. 5 Lakhs** require Finance approval.
3. **Vendor Contracts**: New vendor contracts must go through the Procurement team. Contracts above **Rs. 10 Lakhs** require Legal review.
4. **Training Budget**: Each department has a training allocation of **2% of department budget**. Unused training budget cannot be carried forward.
5. **Contingency**: A **5% contingency buffer** is maintained at the company level for unplanned expenses.

## Budget Variance Reporting

- Managers must submit monthly budget utilization reports by the **5th of each month**.
- Variances exceeding **10%** of the quarterly plan require a written explanation to Finance.
- Variances exceeding **20%** trigger an automatic review by the CFO.

## Cost Reduction Targets

The leadership team has set the following cost efficiency targets for FY 2026-27:

- Reduce software licensing costs by **15%** through vendor consolidation.
- Reduce travel expenses by **10%** by leveraging video conferencing for non-critical meetings.
- Improve engineering productivity by **8%** through developer tooling investments.

## Confidentiality

Budget details, variance reports, and cost targets are management confidential. Sharing specific budget numbers with individual contributors or external parties is a violation of the information classification policy.

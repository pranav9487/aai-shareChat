access_level: management

Management briefing — for people managers and above. Decisions summarized here are confidential and preliminary until announced.

Budget planning: department leads submit draft budgets by the 15th; finance consolidates before the steering committee reviews variances above 10 percent. Any new recurring spend above 20,000 units requires VP sign-off.

Headcount planning for next year assumes flat overall growth. Requisitions for senior roles route through the talent committee; backfills below senior level are approved inside the department.

Performance calibration guidance asks managers to propose ratings independently before the calibration session, and reminds them that promotion cases require evidence across two consecutive cycles.

## Hiring Freeze Conditions

A company-wide or department-specific hiring freeze may be triggered by:
- Revenue falling below **85%** of quarterly target for two consecutive quarters.
- Department budget utilization exceeding **110%** of the approved budget.
- Strategic decision by the leadership team (communicated via CFO memo).

During a hiring freeze, only critical backfill positions (approved by the CEO) may proceed.

## Internal Transfers

- Employees may apply for internal positions after completing **18 months** in their current role.
- Internal candidates receive priority consideration over external candidates at the same qualification level.
- Transfer between departments requires approval from both the releasing and receiving managers.

## Contractor and Vendor Staff

- Contractor positions must not exceed **15%** of any department's total headcount.
- All contractor engagements above 6 months must be reviewed for conversion to full-time employment.
- Vendor staff are managed through the Procurement team and are not included in headcount targets.

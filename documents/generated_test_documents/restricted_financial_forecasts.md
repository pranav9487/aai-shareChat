## Key Financial Risks

1. **Currency risk**: 35% of revenue is denominated in USD. A 5% INR appreciation would reduce revenue by Rs. 1.7 Crores.
2. **Client concentration**: Top 3 clients account for 28% of total revenue. Loss of any one could impact revenue by Rs. 8-10 Crores.
3. **Pricing pressure**: Competitors (FreshDesk, Zoho) are pricing 15-20% below Nexora for mid-market deals.
4. **Regulatory risk**: New data localization requirements may increase infrastructure costs by Rs. 2 Crores annually.
